# Correctness and expressiveness

This section answers two questions about Dex.

- **Expressiveness**: can Dex express the programs you need?
- **Correctness**: does Dex stay correct when processes, machines, or networks
  fail?

## Expressiveness

The [Expressiveness proof](/concepts/correctness-and-expressiveness/expressiveness-proof)
shows that any program in a small concurrent language, with threads, locks,
timers, and cancellation, can be written as a Dex Flow that behaves the same.
A computer checks every step of the proof. The
[paper](/papers/dex-expressiveness.pdf) has the full argument.

The [Workflow Patterns evaluation](/concepts/correctness-and-expressiveness/workflow-patterns)
rates Dex against the 43 control-flow Workflow Patterns. Researchers use these
patterns to compare workflow languages and engines. Each rating comes with a
runnable example and an integration test.

## Correctness

The [Step guarantees](/concepts/step#guarantees) state what Dex promises when
a Step method fails or runs again. A second proof will show that crashes,
retries, and redelivery do not change what a Flow does.
[Issue 1514](https://github.com/superdurable/dex/issues/1514) tracks it.
