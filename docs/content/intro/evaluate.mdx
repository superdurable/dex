# Evaluate Dex

These pages help you decide whether Dex fits your system. Read them in order, or go straight to your question.

- [Why Dex?](/intro/what-is-dex) shows the problem with an order process that breaks halfway, and how Dex models it as a Flow of Steps.
- [Dex vs. Databases](/intro/compared-to-database) builds the same process on a database, then with queues and an outbox, then as a Flow, and lists the duties that stay with you.
- [Dex and Your Database](/components/your-database) shows how a Step writes to the database you already have, and when to copy Flow data to PostgreSQL.
- [Dex vs. Temporal](/intro/compared-to-durable-execution) maps each core Temporal capability onto Dex, says when Temporal fits better, and ends with other engines.
- [Performance](/intro/performance) gives measured throughput and latency on one node with S3, what it costs to run, and how to repeat a result.
- [Limits](/intro/limits) lists what can surprise a team in production, the release status, and the license.
- [Failover and Deploys](/concepts/failover-and-deploys) shows what happens when the server or a Worker stops, and how to deploy new code while Flows are open.
- [Correctness and Expressiveness](/correctness-and-expressiveness) gives a machine-checked proof of what a crash can and cannot change, and what Dex can express.
