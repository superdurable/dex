# Dex Docs

Dex is a **Durable Execution** platform. A database stores your data; Dex also stores the progress of your process: which Steps ran, what they returned, and what the process is waiting for. After a crash, a restart, or a long wait, the process continues where it stopped.

**Start here:** the [Quick Start](/quick-start). It takes about 10 minutes.

## Pick a path

- **Fix a process that breaks halfway:** [the 20-minute path](/get-started#fix-a-process) through [Why Dex](/intro/what-is-dex), the [Quick Start](/quick-start), and [Port Your Process](/intro/port-your-process).
- **Evaluate Dex:** [Compared to Database](/intro/compared-to-database), [Compared to Existing Durable Execution](/intro/compared-to-durable-execution), [Limits and Sharp Edges](/intro/limits-and-sharp-edges), [Performance and Maturity](/intro/performance-and-maturity), and [Dex and Your Database](/components/your-database).
- **Build a durable agent:** [Durable Agents](/agent-frameworks) and the [Agent Quick Start](/agent-frameworks/quick-start).

To add Dex to a service you already run, see [Your Application: add Dex to a service](/components/application#add-to-a-service). To have a coding agent write Dex code, see [Build with AI](/build-with-ai/dex-developer-skill). [Get Started](/get-started) lists every path with the time it takes.
