# Dex Docs

Dex is a **Durable Execution** platform. A database stores your data; Dex also stores the progress of your process: which Steps ran, what they returned, and what the process is waiting for. After a crash, a restart, or a long wait, the process continues where it stopped.

You write the process as a **Flow** of **Steps**, and a **Worker** in your service runs the Steps.
