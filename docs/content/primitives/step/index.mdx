# Step

**Step** is a key building block of background execution.

- [Basics](/primitives/step/basic) — typed input, the WaitFor/Execute lifecycle, and simple Step decisions
- [Waiting](/primitives/step/waiting-condition) — Condition combinations and reading Condition results after WaitFor
- [Step decision](/primitives/step/step-decision) — movements, close decisions, output, cancellation, and forceCompleteIfChannelsEmpty
- [Step options](/primitives/step/step-options) — timeout, retry, failure policy, durability, heartbeat, and StepOptions override per StepExecution

For a first Flow, read the pages in this order: Basics, Waiting, Step decision, then Step options.

Runnable samples live under examples/go, examples/java, examples/python, examples/typescript, and examples/rust.
