# External Interaction

These patterns cover how a Flow interacts with people and systems outside it. The Flow waits for a decision, notices inactivity, takes a stream of messages, or changes and stops its work on request. The pages go from simple to complex.

## Choose a pattern

- [Decision with Deadline](/design-patterns/external-interaction/decision-with-deadline) — Wait for one of several outside decisions, or a deadline, and let the first one decide what happens next.
- [Idle Timer](/design-patterns/external-interaction/idle-timer) — Reset the inactivity window whenever new activity arrives.
- [Idle Timer with Resolution](/design-patterns/external-interaction/idle-timer-with-resolution) — Record every incoming message until someone resolves the conversation or it goes quiet.
- [Control Long-Running Work](/design-patterns/external-interaction/control-long-running-work) — Change a running loop, such as its batch size, and choose how to stop it: stop the whole run, complete gracefully, or cancel chosen Steps.

When a caller waits for the Flow instead, see [Responsive Update](/design-patterns/responsive-update).
