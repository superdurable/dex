# Design Patterns

Reusable Flow shapes from the Dex examples. Each pattern page explains the problem and the approach, and links to multi-language code.

## Flow control

- [Parallel Steps](/design-patterns/parallel) — Choose static, dynamic, await-all, or first-win concurrent Step coordination.
- [Parallel SubFlows](/design-patterns/parallel-subflows) — Fan out work to SubFlows, bound parent concurrency, partition across parents, and add durable back pressure.
- [Interruptible execution](/design-patterns/interruptible) — Stop or redirect long work gracefully based on durable control signals.
- [Durable Timer](/design-patterns/durable-timer) — Choose cron schedules, reminders, or an inactivity tracker.
- [Deferred Choice](/design-patterns/deferred-choice) — Wait for several events and route on whichever happens first.
- [Critical Section](/design-patterns/critical-section) — Let parallel branches take turns on a sequence of Steps with a Channel token.
- [Cancel with Child Flows](/design-patterns/cancel-case) — Stop the child Flows a Flow started when the Flow is canceled.

## Reliability

- [Failure Handling](/design-patterns/failure-handling) — Recover from execute or wait failures, escalate to an operator, or enforce a business deadline.
- [Polling](/design-patterns/polling) — Wait for an external system in one long-running Step.

## Messaging

- [Drain Channels](/design-patterns/drain-channels) — Finish a Flow after its internal or external Channel messages are processed.
- [Responsive Update](/design-patterns/responsive-update) — Return at a Step boundary, wait for durable state, or stream incremental updates.
- [Inbound Event Loop](/design-patterns/inbound-event-loop) — Take inbound messages until a resolution arrives or the conversation goes quiet.

## Data

- [AttributeMap Partitioning](/design-patterns/attribute-map-partitioning) — Split a growing collection into bounded chunks or hash partitions.
- [Entity Flow with SQL Projection](/design-patterns/entity-store) — Project durable per-entity Flow Attributes into an application-owned SQL table.

Full samples live under **examples/**. Try the HTTP samples from the [examples playground](https://github.com/superdurable/dex/tree/main/examples/playground).
