- **PullWorkerOptions::min_open_slots** makes a pull Worker adaptive when it is below **max_concurrent_tasks**. The Worker keeps between the two counts of slot streams open, both including the reserved RPC slots: it doubles its Step-and-RPC streams when a task takes the last idle one, and every 30 seconds it half-closes half of the idle surplus. An idle adaptive Worker holds only its floor of Server streams. Without the setting the floor equals **max_concurrent_tasks**, which keeps the Worker fixed with unchanged behavior.
- An adaptive Worker clamps **max_concurrent_tasks** above the Server's **matching.maxConcurrentTasksCap** to the cap with a WARN log. It treats a **RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED** registration of a growth stream, or of a reconnect after readiness, as a soft limit, without a **PullRegistrationError**. It retries when one of its own registered streams ends, or at the next 30-second shrink window. While growth batches keep being rejected, it sends at most one growth batch per window.
- **Worker::pull_slot_stream_state** returns a **PullSlotStreamState** snapshot of the slot streams: open, registering, busy, and idle streams, orphaned handlers, the floor and capacities, and grow and shrink counters.
