- **Exact** and **Similar** beside **Folded** in **Graph** choose which consecutive Rounds merge into one range. **Exact**, the default, keeps today's grouping of Rounds with the same Steps. **Similar** also merges Rounds with the same shape, such as one **AgentDecisionStep** and one tool call, and its range names the varying places by a count, such as **AgentDecisionStep → 6 Step types**. The browser remembers the choice.
