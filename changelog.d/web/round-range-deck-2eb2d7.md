section: Changed

- In **Graph** with **Folded** on, a node that folds several Rounds is drawn as a stack of cards. The Round count is its headline, such as **×39 Rounds**, with the Round span and Step count beside it, such as **1–39 · 78 Steps**. A range of one Step type keeps its title, such as **SyncBatch ×900**. Single folded Rounds look as before.
