section: Fixed
---
- The **Connectors** page no longer goes blank after you add a connection to a Connector that needs no credentials, such as **hacker-news-daily**. The page shows its setup form, and saving the form makes the connection Ready.
- The **Connectors** page no longer goes blank for a Flow connection that only Connector Triggers use.
- A connection the **Connectors** page cannot show now displays an error in its own panel. The connection list and the other connections keep working, and the connection can still be removed.
