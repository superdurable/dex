section: Changed
---
- **Timeline** draws the selected event's links as flowing lines, as **Graph** does for a selected Step. A line comes in from the event it came from and goes out to each event it leads to, with an arrow at the target. When a linked event is out of view, its line runs to the lane edge, and the marker there scrolls to it. A linked event that **Filter** hides still shows, faded. With the system's reduced motion setting, the lines stay still.
