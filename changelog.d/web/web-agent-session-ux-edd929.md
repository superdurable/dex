---
section: Changed
---
- In the **Conversation** tab message box, Enter sends the message, or queues it while a turn runs, and Shift+Enter adds a line. Enter that commits input method text does not send.
- The **Conversation** tab follows new messages while you are at the newest one, and offers **Jump to latest** when you have scrolled up. A message longer than about 30 lines shows its start with **Show more**.
- When a tool call starts waiting for approval or an answer, the **Conversation** tab scrolls its controls into view above the message box. The waiting status chip jumps to the call, and a waiting call shows less of its arguments, so its buttons stay close.
