---
section: Changed
---
- In the **Conversation** tab message box, Enter sends the message, or queues it while a turn runs, and Shift+Enter adds a line. Enter that commits input method text does not send. On a touch-only device, such as a phone, Enter adds a line and the button sends.
- The **Conversation** tab follows new messages while you are at the newest one, and offers **Jump to latest** when you have scrolled up. A message longer than about 30 lines shows its start with **Show more**.
- When a tool call starts waiting for approval or an answer while you are at the newest item, the **Conversation** tab scrolls its controls into view above the message box. The waiting status chip jumps to the call, and a waiting call shows less of its arguments, so its buttons stay close. The tab does not move the view when you have scrolled up or are working in another waiting call.
