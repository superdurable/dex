- A completed Execute on the run page's **Graph** shows the Step decision it
  returned in place of **Completed**: the next Step types, such as
  **→ ResumeTurn, CallTool** with **+N** for more, or **Dead end**,
  **Graceful complete**, **Force complete**, **Force fail**, **Complete when
  channels are empty**, or the canceled Step types. Each has its own icon and
  color, and the tooltip lists the whole decision.
