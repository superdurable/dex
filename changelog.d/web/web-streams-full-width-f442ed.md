section: Changed
---
- A Flow's **Streams** tab uses the full width of the page, and its messages flow in the page instead of a fixed-height box with its own scroll bar. The page keeps your place while new entries arrive above it, and **Back to latest** stays on screen while you scroll. A live transcript moves the page only after you scroll down to its end; until then, **N new · Back to latest** shows how much text has arrived below.
