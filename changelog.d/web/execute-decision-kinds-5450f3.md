section: Changed
---
- A completed Execute in **Graph** names its Step decision by the SDK decision name, such as `GoTo` **×2**, `DeadEnd`, `ForceFail`, or `ForceCompleteIfChannelsEmpty` with its Channel names, instead of the next Step names, which the edges already show. Canceled Steps show as `CancelSteps` **×N** beside any decision, and the tooltip still lists every Step.
