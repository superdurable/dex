- **Folded** on the run page's **Graph** folds repeated Rounds of Steps.
  Consecutive Rounds with the same Steps fold into one node, such as
  **Rounds 1–100** or **SyncBatch ×900**. It is off by default and the
  browser remembers the choice. The latest Round and Rounds with a failed or
  waiting Step stay open, **Show Steps** and **Show Rounds** open a folded
  node and bring it into view, and selecting an event inside one opens it. A
  short card introduces **Folded** the first time **Graph** opens.
- The **Graph** mini map is always shown at the bottom right, colored by Step
  status, and moves the view on click or drag. A small button hides it to a
  map button, and the browser remembers the choice. It replaces the
  **Mini Map** button.
- **Graph** nodes drop their borders for soft shadows, rounded corners, and
  tinted WaitFor and Execute chips; edges are thinner, and edges into a
  waiting Step are dashed.
