section: Added

- **Folded** on the run page's **Graph** groups repeated Rounds of Steps into
  one node, such as **SyncBatch ×900** or **Round 3**. It is off by default
  and the browser remembers the choice. The latest Round and Rounds with a
  failed Step stay open, **Show Steps** and **Hide Steps** open and fold a
  Round, and selecting an event inside a folded Round opens it. A short card
  introduces **Folded** the first time **Graph** opens.
- The **Graph** mini map is always shown at the bottom right, colored by Step
  status, and moves the view on click or drag. A small button hides it to a
  map button, and the browser remembers the choice. It replaces the
  **Mini Map** button.
- **Graph** nodes drop their borders for soft shadows, rounded corners, and
  tinted WaitFor and Execute chips; edges are thinner, and edges into a
  waiting Step are dashed.
