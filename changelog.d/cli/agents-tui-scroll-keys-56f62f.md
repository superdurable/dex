section: Changed
---
- In the **dexcli agents-tui** chat, **Up** and **Down** on an empty input
  scroll the messages one line, so the mouse wheel scrolls in terminals that
  send it as arrow keys. **Ctrl-P** and **Ctrl-N** recall earlier messages.
  **Up** and **Down** keep recalling while the input holds an unedited
  recalled message, and move between the lines of a typed message. The plan's
  steps open with **Ctrl-T** instead of **Ctrl-P**.
